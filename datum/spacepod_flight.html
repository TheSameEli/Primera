spacepod_flight
/datum/spacepod_flight

Pixel flight with relative thrusters, adapted from the Yogstation/Paradise spacepod physics. Native Move owns collision and sector transitions; this datum owns velocity and visual offsets.

Vars

angular_velocityClockwise degrees per second.
brakesBraking uses the same finite thrusters as manual input.
camera_offsetsPer-occupant camera contribution, without retaining clients.
desired_headingLast requested mouse heading; null means no target.
headingClockwise degrees, with north at zero.
input_directionRelative thrust requested for the next physics tick only.
last_step_bumpedA real obstacle was hit during the last native hull step.
last_step_obstacleOnly retained until the checked Move returns, including directional window exits.
offset_xSub-tile displacement relative to the checked native origin.
offset_ySub-tile displacement relative to the checked native origin.
podThe vehicle owns and deletes its flight controller.
turboDoubles linear thruster output and the speed limit; energy follows actual thrust.
velocity_xWorld-space velocity in tiles per second.
velocity_yWorld-space velocity in tiles per second.
