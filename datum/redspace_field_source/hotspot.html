hotspot
/datum/redspace_field_source/hotspot

A stable local anomaly: a rift trace, an entity footprint or a faulty bluespace object. Behaves like a static source but exists as its own type so events and the journal can reason about persistent zones separately from debug contributions. Growing hotspots advance in discrete one-minute steps so their field cache is not rebuilt every machinery tick.
