redspace_field_observer
/datum/redspace_field_observer

Shared turf subscription lifecycle. The caller chooses the turf (including whether a contained item can observe) and handles movement/gameplay effects. The callback asks the caller to resample after initialization or ChangeTurf; it receives the replacement turf, or null after subsystem initialization.
