khaaroot_mutations
/datum/khaaroot_mutations

Owns one exact Khaaroot role's mutations and its current body's actions.

Vars

actionsActions owned by this controller, recreated concealed on each body.
antag_refExact role that owns this controller, without retaining a removed membership.
body_refCurrent compatible body; role transfer explicitly rebinds it.
specializationThrall-only permanent choice: tendril, gland, or molt; the source receives all three.
