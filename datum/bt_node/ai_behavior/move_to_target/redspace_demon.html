redspace_demon
/datum/bt_node/ai_behavior/move_to_target/redspace_demon

Keeps the JPS loop from retrying after the demon has already reached melee range, and swaps to instant avoidance movement near a visible target so shoves and strafing cannot stall the demon. When JPS gives up on a route the demon walks straight at its target instead of standing still: the direct approach carries it to whatever blocks the way and [attack_obstructions] clears it, so a sealer walled in behind cover is eventually reached and broken through.

Vars

approach_rangeDistance at which the demon drops JPS and moves directly at its target.
approach_targetThe target the direct approach was started against; a new target resets it.
direct_approachSet while the smart route is exhausted and the demon walks straight at its target.

Procs
