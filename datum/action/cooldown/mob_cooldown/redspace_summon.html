redspace_summon
/datum/action/cooldown/mob_cooldown/redspace_summon

Summons a lesser demon to fight for the Ravager. The type is chosen from a radial menu.

Vars

summon_optionsTypepath -> (limit, icon, icon_state, pixel_x, pixel_y) for the radial summon menu.
summon_typeDemon type chosen from the radial menu.
summoned_demonsSummoned demons that are still alive, used for the per-type limits.

Procs
