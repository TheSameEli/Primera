telephone
/obj/item/telephone

A tethered receiver which relays its holder's audible speech through an established call.

Vars

attached_toThe base owns this handset; its cord limits ordinary movement.
holderThe living mob currently holding the receiver in a hand.
raisedRaising the handset enables its microphone and brings the incoming voice to the holder's ear.
telephone_beamThe cord follows the holder or the handset lying on the floor.

Procs

attack_self_secondaryReserves the other hand to cover only the microphone, leaving incoming speech audible.
check_tetherKeeps container handling, but never docks a handset because its distance changed.
clear_holderReleases the holder, movement listeners and cord before recall or deletion.
cord_reachesWhether two endpoints fit within the cord on the same z-level.
on_tether_pre_moveStops an ordinary step or dragging either endpoint before the cord is overstretched.
on_tether_z_moveVertical movement must check the cord before forceMove separates its endpoints.
update_beamDraws the cord while off-hook, including between holders during a handoff.
